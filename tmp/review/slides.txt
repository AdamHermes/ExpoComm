ppt/slides/slide17.xml
Discussion
How our single-seed reproduction compares with the paper's claims.
✓
Held up
One peer (K = 1) matches or beats K = ⌈log₂ N⌉ in 5 of 6 MAgent runs.
ExpoComm is top two in all 6 IMP scenarios.
Same tactics as the paper: corner the prey, win the front line.
~
Differed
Our K = ⌈log₂ N⌉ Pursuit runs reach 61–70 (paper: ≈25–50).
IMP: K = 1 is never our best (paper: best on Correlated).
Transfer: ER K = ⌈log₂ N⌉ wins 22 match-ups, ExpoComm 13.
!
Caveats
One seed per run, so no confidence intervals.
Large-map transfer is mostly decided by the starting side.
Ablation, CommFormer and DGN+TarMAC not run.
Core claim holds on MAgent; IMP and transfer gains are small at one seed.
